Liberating the value of gift cards into cold hard cash!

Our mission is simple. We’re founded upon the principal that it shouldn’t be difficult to free up the value of your unwanted gift cards so you can spend your cash where you want, when you want. It’s that simple.

We value customer service and strive to make your experience with us as simple as possible. We love the communities we serve and we’re proud to support personalities, athletes and organizations that showcase the best California has to offer.

Card City wasn’t built in a day

Since we were founded in 2012, we’ve taken our mission seriously. We help our Cardcitizens free up their unused gift card values. We’ve grown beyond our humble beginnings in Fresno to 2 locations serving Fresno and Sacramento. Along the way, we’ve learned a lot about how to best serve our customers to make converting your gift cards into cash as convenient a process as possible.

Committed to the community

We know we wouldn’t exist without support from the communities we serve, so we’re active participants in doing what we can to make the areas we serve vibrant and healthy.
